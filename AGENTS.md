# Loupe premium exhibit standard

For every new exhibit, major exhibit redesign, or substantial exhibit extension, read and follow [`docs/premium-exhibit-standard.md`](docs/premium-exhibit-standard.md) before ideation, planning, or implementation.

Treat that document as an acceptance contract, not optional inspiration. The quality floor is the research depth, coherence, honesty, completeness, and refinement demonstrated by **Atlas of Worlds**. This does not authorize copying its interface, visual language, 3D treatment, information architecture, or feature set. Each exhibit must derive its own form from its subject and visitor promise.

In particular:

- establish a clear curatorial thesis and intended visitor transformation;
- research authoritative primary sources and preserve provenance;
- distinguish observation, interpretation, reconstruction, and illustration honestly;
- design one coherent end-to-end experience instead of a collection of disconnected effects;
- ensure every visible control and promised mode has meaningful behavior;
- finish responsive, accessible, loading, empty, fallback, and error states;
- perform iterative visual and interaction QA at representative desktop and mobile viewports; and
- provide current verification evidence before describing an exhibit as complete or premium.

If delivery constraints would force a material downgrade, state the trade-off explicitly and ask the user to choose the scope. Never silently lower the standard, substitute placeholders, or use unsupported claims to simulate completeness.
